Get Involved

Join our mission to keep the sanctuary safe, beautiful, and thriving for dogs and their humans.

At Potts Dog Sanctuary, we're passionate about creating a safe and beautiful environment for our canine companions and their human friends. There are several ways you can contribute to our mission and be part of our vibrant community.

Volunteer

Sign up to join upcoming maintenance parties and events. Your involvement can range from park clean-ups to special projects—every effort counts in making Ott's Dog Sanctuary a better place!

Sign Up to Volunteer

Make a Donation

Support us financially. Your tax-free donations help us improve the park's facilities and much more. Please consider a gift of $1.00 per dog for each visit. For example, for one dog visiting two days per week, a gift of $100 per year would be appropriate.

Make a Donation

Be Part of Our Team

We're looking for enthusiastic individuals to fill key volunteer roles. Right now, we especially need someone with experience in grant writing.

Join the Team

Spread the Word

Become an Ambassador! Share your experiences at Potts Dog Sanctuary with friends, family, and on social media. The more people know us, the greater our impact can be.
