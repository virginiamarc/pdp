Frequently Asked Questions

Visiting the Sanctuary

What are your hours?
We’re open Wednesday–Sunday, 7:30 AM to sunset. Closed Monday–Tuesday.

Do I need an appointment?
General visits do not require an appointment. Some volunteer activities may.

Where are you located?
Ruskin, Florida. A Google Maps link is available in the footer.

About the Dogs

Are the dogs adoptable?
Many are adoptable; others are long‑term sanctuary residents.

Do you take owner surrenders?
Case‑by‑case depending on space, temperament, and medical needs.

How do you ensure safety?
Fully fenced grounds, trained volunteers, and temperament‑based grouping.

Volunteering

How can I volunteer?
Visit our Get Involved page for available roles.

Is there an age requirement?
Volunteers must be 16+; under 18 requires a guardian.

Do I need experience?
No — training is provided.

Donations & Support

How can I donate?
Through our secure Donorbox form.

Are donations tax‑deductible?
We will notify donors once nonprofit registration is finalized.

Can I donate supplies?
Yes — contact us for our current needs list.

Safety & Policies

Are outside dogs allowed?
No, for safety reasons.

Can I host an event?
Small community events may be possible — contact us.

Is photography allowed?
Yes, but avoid flash around sensitive dogs.
